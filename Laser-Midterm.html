Basic Components of Laser

Midterm Review · Laser in Dentistry · 2026–2027

① How laser light is produced
  • 1Energy (electricity or light) pumps the electrons up to an excited level.
  • 2That level is unstable, so the electron falls back and releases a photon on its own.
  • 3If a photon hits an excited atom first, the fall is forced and gives a second, identical photon.
  • 4The two photons trigger more atoms → chain reaction → the light is amplified.
💡 طاقة ترفع الإلكترون، هو ينزل ويطلّع فوتون، والفوتون يحفّز غيره، فالضوء يتضاعف.
② Laser light vs ordinary light
  • 1Bulb: many colors, spreads in all directions, waves out of step.
  • 2Laser: one color, waves in step, parallel, one direction.
💡 الليزر ضوء منظّم، واللمبة العادية ضوء مبعثر.
③ Follow the beam inside the device
  • 1The pumping source energizes the active medium, which sits in the optical chamber.
  • 2Two parallel mirrors bounce the photons back and forth, so the light gets stronger.
  • 3The beam leaves through the partially reflecting mirror. With two 100% mirrors, energy would keep building until the tube is destroyed.
  • 4Heat is a by-product, so a cooling system (air or water) is needed.
  • 5The beam then passes through the delivery system to the handpiece and the tissue.
💡 الوسط الفعّال بياخد الطاقة، المراية بتقوّي الضوء، المراية الجزئية بتسيبه يخرج، والتبريد بيشيل الحرارة.
④ How the beam is given to the tissue
  • 1Contact: the tip touches the tissue. Non-contact: the beam is pointed from a distance.
  • 2Continuous: constant power, so heat keeps building with no cooling time and more post-operative discomfort.
  • 3Pulsed: the beam pauses between pulses, so the tissue can cool (thermal relaxation).
💡 المستمر بيسخّن ومفيش راحة للنسيج، والنبضي بيدّي النسيج وقت يبرّد.

Laser-Tissue Interaction

Midterm Review · Laser in Dentistry · 2026–2027

① What the tissue does with laser energy
  • 1Absorbed → the energy does the work. This is the most important interaction.
  • 2Transmitted → passes through with no effect.
  • 3Scattered → spreads to adjacent, unwanted tissue.
  • 4Reflected → bounces off the surface (only a small part).
  • 5Patient and all staff wear safety glasses with a filter for that specific wavelength.
💡 الامتصاص هو اللي بيعمل التأثير، والباقي يا بيعدّي أو بيتشتت أو بيرتد.
② Why wavelength decides the target
  • 1Each wavelength is absorbed by its own chromophore (المادة اللي بتمتص الليزر).
  • 2Pigments (melanin, hemoglobin) absorb the shorter wavelengths: diodes, Nd:YAG.
  • 3Water and hydroxyapatite absorb the longer wavelengths: Erbium, CO2.
  • 4Shorter (near-infrared) wavelengths go deeper than longer (mid-infrared) ones.
💡 الليزر بيشتغل على اللي بيمتصه: الصبغات للأطوال القصيرة، والمية والـ hydroxyapatite للأطوال الطويلة.
③ Four effects inside the tissue
  • 1Fluorescence: the tissue glows with a second wavelength; used to detect caries.
  • 2Photothermal: absorbed energy becomes heat; cuts or removes soft tissue.
  • 3Photodisruptive: a shock wave breaks up hard tissue.
  • 4Photobiomodulation: low-level energy stimulates healing.
💡 أربع تأثيرات: توهّج، حرارة، موجة صدمة، وتحفيز للشفا.
④ Photothermal: more heat, more damage
  • 1As temperature rises: hyperthermia → coagulation → vaporization (tissue turns to water and CO2) → carbonization (charring).
  • 2Carbonization is irreversible damage: inflammation, pain and edema.
💡 كل ما الحرارة تزيد التأثير يبقى أشد، وآخره التفحّم وده مش بيرجع.
⑤ Photodisruptive: how hard tissue is removed
  • 1Extremely high power hits the water inside the tissue.
  • 2The water expands suddenly → a thermo-mechanical shock wave (the popping sound).
  • 3Enamel and bone are pulverized, not vaporized.
  • 4Decayed areas hold more water, and the laser also kills bacteria there.
💡 الليزر بيفجّر المية اللي جوه السن فتتكسّر الأنسجة الصلبة، مش بتتبخّر.
⑥ Photobiomodulation
  • 1Also called LLLT (Low Level Laser Therapy) or biostimulation.
  • 2Speeds healing, increases circulation, reduces edema and pain.
💡 تحفيز بدون حرارة: شفا أسرع ودورة دموية أحسن وتورّم وألم أقل.

Classification of Lasers

Midterm Review · Laser in Dentistry · 2026–2027

① Four ways to classify lasers
  • 1Power: high-power lasers heat the tissue (surgery); low-level lasers do not heat it, they only stimulate it.
  • 2Wavelength: from ultraviolet to far infrared.
  • 3Active medium: gas, liquid, solid or semiconductor.
  • 4Application: soft-tissue lasers vs hard-tissue lasers.
💡 التصنيف بالقدرة، بالطول الموجي، بالوسط الفعّال، وبالاستخدام.
② The key idea: the chromophore decides the target
  • 1Argon, Diode, Nd:YAG are absorbed by pigments → they work on gingiva and mucosa (soft tissue).
  • 2Er:YAG and CO2 are absorbed by water and hydroxyapatite → they also reach enamel, dentin and bone.
💡 اللي بيمتص الليزر هو اللي بيحدد هو هيشتغل على نسيج رخو بس ولا على الصلب كمان.
③ The three soft-tissue lasers in one line each
  • 1Argon: visible blue-green light that hemoglobin absorbs → stops bleeding well; the one used to cure resin.
  • 2Diode: small, portable and the cheapest; also used for whitening and mouth ulcers.
  • 3Nd:YAG: pulsed; great hemostasis, but the deepest penetration, so a risk to bone and pulp.
💡 Argon للـ resin، Diode الرخيص والصغير، وNd:YAG بيوقّف النزيف بس أعمق واحد في الاختراق. التفاصيل في الـ Study Sheet.

Erbium and CO2 Lasers

Midterm Review · Laser in Dentistry · 2026–2027

① What makes Erbium and CO2 special
  • 1Both are absorbed by water and hydroxyapatite, so they work on hard and soft tissue, unlike Argon, Diode and Nd:YAG.
💡 الاتنين بيمتصهم المية والـ hydroxyapatite فبيشتغلوا على الأنسجة الصلبة والرخوة.
② Erbium: the hard-tissue laser
  • 1Two types: Er:YAG and Er,Cr:YSGG. Solid laser, pulsed.
  • 2Hydroxyapatite absorption → removes caries, prepares enamel and dentin, cuts bone. Water absorption → also works on soft tissue.
  • 3Easy on the patient: negligible penetration (pulp is safe), no anesthesia for caries removal, less vibration than the high-speed drill, antimicrobial, clean sharp margins.
💡 الـ Erbium بيقطع السن والعظم من غير ما يأذي الـ pulp وغالبًا من غير تخدير.
③ CO2: the invisible beam
  • 1Gas laser (CO2 + nitrogen + helium), far infrared, used in non-contact mode only.
  • 2Its beam is invisible, so a visible red Helium-Nitrogen laser travels with it as a pointer.
  • 3The two beams must be aligned (check before use), otherwise they hit different spots and healthy tissue is destroyed.
💡 شعاع الـ CO2 مش بيتشاف، فمعاه ليزر أحمر كمؤشر، ولازم يتشيّك إنهم على نفس المحور.
④ CO2 modes
  • 1Early CO2 was continuous: long pulses cause crazing of tooth structure, so it was poor for hard tissue.
  • 2Now pulsed / superpulsed: very short, very strong peaks with rest times between them, so the tissue cools and thermal injury is less.
💡 النبضي بيدّي النسيج وقت يبرّد فالإصابة الحرارية أقل.
⑤ CO2 in soft tissue
  • 1Water absorbs it, so it cuts and coagulates soft tissue, only at the surface (shallow penetration).
  • 2Result: excellent hemostasis, a dry field with instant visual feedback, no edema.
  • 3Cautions: hydroxyapatite absorbs it far more than erbium, so shield the adjacent tooth; no tactile feedback; temporary black/brown carbon residue.
💡 بيقطع الأنسجة الرخوة على السطح بس ونضيف، بس لازم نحمي السن اللي جنبه.
